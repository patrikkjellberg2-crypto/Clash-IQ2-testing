How Clash IQ works

The goal is simple: you should not have to hunt through multiple screens to understand your Clash of Clans account.

01

Sign in

Use your Google account to access your personal Clash IQ dashboard.

02

Connect your player

Link your Clash of Clans player tag to your Clash IQ account.

03

Explore your data

Open your clan, Player Card, progress, rankings, wars and historical information.

04

Use the app

The Android app gives you the same core information in a dedicated mobile experience.

Inside the Clash IQ app

Everything your Clash account needs — clearly explained.

Clash IQ is more than a profile viewer. The app is designed as a complete information and analytics hub for players and clans. The website can expose the same personal features after you sign in.

🏰

Clan Dashboard

See your clan's key information in one place: members, Town Halls, trophies, roles, activity and other available clan data.

👥

Members

Browse the complete roster and open information about individual members. Clan position and available player data can be viewed without searching manually.

⚔️

Next War

Get a clear view of the current or upcoming war, including both clans, player counts, attacks, stars, destruction and the current war state.

📜

War History

Review previous wars and compare results over time. See wins, losses, stars and destruction instead of relying on memory.

👤

Your Player Card

After signing in and linking your player tag, your own profile can show Town Hall, trophies, league, clan position, experience, donations and Builder Base information.

📈

Your Progress

Get a dedicated progress dashboard showing overall development, buildings, heroes, troops and equipment, current upgrades, remaining work and historical progress.

🏆

Rankings

Keep clan rank, Ranked placement, local ranking and world ranking separate. A ranking is shown only when the underlying source provides verified data.

📅

Ranked Season History

Follow Ranked seasons over time and compare league, placement and other verified season information instead of seeing only the current season.

🏰

Clan Capital

See Capital-related information and seasonal activity alongside the rest of your clan data.

🌍

World & Local Data

When verified leaderboard information exists, Clash IQ can show global and local positions separately from your clan rank and Ranked placement.

📊

Historical Insights

Keep useful information over time so you can compare seasons, performance and development instead of looking at one isolated snapshot.

🔐

Personal Web Dashboard

Sign in with Google on the website, connect your player tag and access your own clan information, Player Card, progress, rankings and history from the browser.

How the plans work

Free is the real Clash IQ product, not a demo. Premium is for individual players who want deeper personal history and analytics. Clan Premium unlocks advanced non-AI features for the whole clan. Clan Leader Premium is optional and adds the AI Coach, AI War Planner and AI-powered leadership analysis. Clan Premium follows the clan, while Leader Premium does not automatically give every member leader privileges.
